Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept custom development projects?
Yes, we provide full-process custom development services from concept to launch, including enterprise Gamification applications, VR exhibition halls, and more.
How long does it take to develop a game?
It depends on the scope and type of game. Casual games typically take 1-2 months, while mid-to-heavy RPG projects may require 6 months or more.
How is after-sales maintenance handled?
We provide at least 6 months of free technical maintenance to ensure stable operation after launch, with rapid response to bug fixes.
